# Temporal Knowledge Graph Reasoning

[Home](../README.md) · [Datasets & Benchmarks](../resources/datasets-and-benchmarks.md) · [Libraries](../resources/libraries.md)

Reasoning over time-stamped knowledge graph facts, including future event forecasting, temporal link prediction, and multi-hop logical queries. The list highlights papers from NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR, with closely related temporal KG alignment work in a separate section.
